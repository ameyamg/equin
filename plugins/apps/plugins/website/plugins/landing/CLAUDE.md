# landing

The homepage, as one band per plugin: the hero, the Try it band, the layers,
the contact block. The live demo's frame (`screenshot`) and the story line
(`story-link`) are hidden in `section.jsonc`: Try it's Open the demo opens the
demo page instead, its box growing out of the button (`morphDemoFrame` with a
`from` element), and the Story lives in the Learn menu and the footer.

The bands are separate plugins because each is a separable piece of the page's
argument, but their ORDER is not theirs to decide — it is authored in
`config/apps/website/shell/section.jsonc`. Add a band and it appears at the end
until you place it there.

Every band composes `WebsiteBand` (from the shell), which owns the site's one
reading measure and the hairline between bands. A band that pads and centres
itself will drift out of line with the rest of the site.
